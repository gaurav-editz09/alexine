01 Our story

We cook with fire, because fire remembers.

Ember & Spice began with a simple obsession: the moment a spice hits a live flame. That split second when cardamom turns sweet, chilli turns smoky, and everything you thought you knew about Indian food changes.

We source whole spices from small growers along the Malabar coast, temper them daily, and cook over English oak and Binchotan. Nothing is rushed. Nothing is hidden. The kitchen is open, the fire is real, and the menu follows the season — not the other way round.

The room is small on purpose. Thirty-four seats, a handful of tables, and eight stools at the counter where you can watch every flame, every turn, every plate leave the pass.
