01 Our story

We cook with fire, because fire remembers.

Ember & Spice began with a simple obsession: the moment a spice hits a live flame. That split second when cardamom turns sweet, chilli turns smoky, and everything you thought you knew about Indian food changes.

We source whole spices from small growers along the Malabar coast, temper them daily, and cook over English oak and Binchotan. Nothing is rushed. Nothing is hidden. The kitchen is open, the fire is real, and the menu follows the season — not the other way round.

The room is small on purpose. Thirty-four seats, a handful of tables, and eight stools at the counter where you can watch every flame, every turn, every plate leave the pass.

05 The experience

An evening, paced by the fire.

Dinner at Ember & Spice is a sequence, not a menu. It begins with something cold and sharp, moves through smoke and heat, and ends with something sweet and burnt. The pace is deliberate. The room is small. The fire decides.

01

First light

Aperitif at the bar. Chilled oysters, kokum, pink pepper — something cold before the heat.

02

The hearth

Eight seats at the counter, front row to the fire and the pass. Ask the chef what just came off.

03

Afterglow

Burnt honey, aged rum, and the last of the evening. The room stays open until the fire goes out.

06 Find us

Come for the fire. Stay for the last train.

Tucked off a Shoreditch side street, behind a dark green door and a curtain of smoke. Walk-ins are welcome at the bar; the dining room is best booked ahead.
