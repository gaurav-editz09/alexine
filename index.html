01 Our story

We cook with fire, because fire remembers.

Ember & Spice began with a simple obsession: the moment sattu meets a live flame. That split second when the crust blisters, mustard oil turns nutty, and the food of Bihar tastes like it should.

We source sattu from small mills around Purnea, cold-press our mustard oil, and cook over mango wood and charcoal. Litti is roasted slowly, chokha is smoked over the same coals, and the mutton is left alone until it gives in. Nothing is rushed. Nothing is hidden.

The room is small on purpose. Thirty-four seats, a handful of tables, and eight stools at the counter where you can watch every flame, every turn, every plate leave the pass.

05 The experience

An evening, paced by the fire.

Dinner at Ember & Spice is a sequence, not a menu. It begins with something cold and sharp, moves through smoke and heat, and ends with something sweet and burnt. The pace is deliberate. The room is small. The fire decides.

01

First light

Aperitif at the bar. Chilled sattu sharbat, kokum, roasted cumin — something cold before the heat.

02

The hearth

Eight seats at the counter, front row to the fire and the pass. Ask the chef what just came off.

03

Afterglow

Malpua, jaggery, aged rum, and the last of the evening. The room stays open until the fire goes out.

06 Find us

Come for the fire. Stay for the last train.

Tucked off Station Road, behind a dark green door and a curtain of smoke. Walk-ins are welcome at the bar; the dining room is best booked ahead.
